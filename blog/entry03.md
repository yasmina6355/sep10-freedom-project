#### BrainStorming 
I initially began brainstorming ideas for future architecture innovations. I was considering how some of the potential future technology might assist in the development and advancement of architecture  in the near future. A 3D printer was one of the ideas that sprang to mind during my brainstorming session. 3D printing for architecture entails employing large-scale printers to produce structures layer by layer from materials such as concrete, metal, and polymers. It can lead to faster construction, less material waste, and lower labor expenses. Another excellent idea was robotic construction. Robotic systems in construction can help with operations such as bricklaying, welding, and panel assembly. Robots can work around the clock, with high precision, and minimize the need for human labor in dangerous activities. This can help to increase building pace, improve precision, and reduce human errors. Robots can also work in dangerous situations, increasing safety on building sites. Finally, I considered use of AI in design and planning. Simply said, AI algorithms can help with architectural design by evaluating large volumes of data to develop the best building layouts, improve space consumption, and predict environmental aspects. It's a faster and easier way to develop things.
